Publications and citation
=========================

If you use SIMPLE in a publication, cite the method papers that apply to your
work. The software itself is archived on Zenodo; the
badge in the repository README links to the current version.

Method papers
-------------

#. C. G. Albert, S. V. Kasilov, W. Kernbichler, Symplectic integration with
   non-canonical quadrature for guiding-center orbits in magnetic confinement
   devices, *J. Comput. Phys.* 403, 109065 (2020),
   https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2019.109065
#. C. G. Albert, S. V. Kasilov, W. Kernbichler, Accelerated methods for direct
   computation of fusion alpha particle losses within stellarator
   optimization, *J. Plasma Phys.* 86, 815860201 (2020),
   https://doi.org/10.1017/S0022377820000203
#. C. G. Albert, R. Buchholz, S. V. Kasilov, W. Kernbichler, K. Rath, Alpha
   particle confinement metrics based on orbit classification in
   stellarators, *J. Plasma Phys.* 89, 955890301 (2023),
   https://doi.org/10.1017/S0022377823000351
#. C. G. Albert, G. Grassler, J. Lion, J.-F. Lobsien, J. Schatzlmayr,
   F. Warmer, Symplectic methods for optimization of wall heat loads by
   fusion alpha particles in stellarator reactors, *Nucl. Mater. Energy* 48,
   102200 (2026), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nme.2026.102200

Related work used by SIMPLE
---------------------------

* J. D. Meiss, R. D. Hazeltine, Canonical coordinates for guiding center
  particles, *Phys. Fluids B* 2, 2563 (1990), https://doi.org/10.1063/1.859380
* R. G. Littlejohn, Variational principles of guiding centre motion,
  *J. Plasma Phys.* 29, 111 (1983), https://doi.org/10.1017/S002237780000060X
* M. Landreman, M. Czekanski, A. Giuliani, B. Jang, R. Conlin, Bayesian
  optimization of stellarator alpha-particle confinement using data-informed
  parameter spaces and dimensionality reduction, arXiv:2606.19523 (2026),
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19523 (segmented loss objective of the GPU
  backend)
* M. Landreman, E. Paul, Magnetic fields with precise quasisymmetry for
  plasma confinement, *Phys. Rev. Lett.* 128, 035001 (2022),
  https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.128.035001 (equilibria used in the
  tutorials)
